# OneVerse — All-in-One Super App 🌐

> Connect. Pay. Create. Live. — All in one place.

OneVerse is a super app UI concept designed for the next generation 
of social interaction. It brings together everything users need — 
social feeds, real-time messaging, digital payments, and live 
streaming — into a single, seamless mobile experience.

---

## 📱 Screens

| Screen | Feature |
|--------|---------|
| 🏠 Splash | Brand onboarding & login |
| 💬 Messages | Inbox, group & direct chats |
| 📸 Feed | Post photos & updates |
| 💸 Wallet | Pay, send, receive & transactions |
| 📡 Live room | Go live, watch & interact in real time |
| 👤 Profile | Account, settings & saved content |

---

## ✨ Features

- **Social Feed** — Share posts, photos and moments with your community
- **Messaging** — Direct and group conversations in real time
- **Wallet** — Send and receive money, scan QR, view transaction history
- **Live room** — Go live or watch creators broadcast in real time
- **Profile & Settings** — Manage your identity, followers and preferences
